Website statistics
==================

We use **Umami Cloud** to understand how people use the H2sim documentation
and which examples are useful. Statistics include page views, referring
websites, browser and device categories, and approximate visitor locations.
Locations are estimated from network information; VPNs and shared networks
can make them inaccurate. We do not request your precise device location.

The analytics tracker uses no cookies. We exclude URL query strings and
fragments from page-view addresses and respect the browser's **Do Not Track**
setting. Local documentation previews are excluded by the domain restriction.
We do not supply names, email addresses, or other custom visitor identifiers
to Umami.

Statistics are viewed through the project's Umami account. Visits blocked by
browser settings or content blockers may not appear in the counts.

For details, see the `Umami FAQ <https://docs.umami.is/docs/faq>`_ and
`tracker configuration <https://docs.umami.is/docs/tracker-configuration>`_.
For questions about this website, see :doc:`community`.
